Refund Policy

Last Updated: 19 September 2026

1. Introduction

This Refund Policy ("Policy") applies to all transactions made through the Universe Wallet platform operated by [আপনার কোম্পানির নাম] ("we", "us", or "our"), accessible at https://universe-wallet.vercel.app and through our Android application.

By using our services, you ("User", "you") agree to the terms outlined in this Refund Policy. Please read it carefully before making any transaction.

2. Scope of This Policy

This Refund Policy covers the following types of transactions:

  • Money Deposits — Amounts added to your wallet
  • Money Transfers — Amounts sent to other users
  • Bill Payments — Payments made through our gateway
  • Service Charges — Fees deducted by Universe Wallet
  • Failed Transactions — Transactions that did not complete

3. Refund Eligibility

You may be eligible for a refund if:

  • The transaction failed but money was deducted from your account
  • You were charged twice for the same transaction (duplicate payment)
  • The amount was debited but never credited to the recipient
  • You made an unauthorized transaction (with valid proof)
  • The service was not delivered as promised

4. Refund Request Timeframe

Refund requests must be submitted within the following timeframes:

Transaction Type Request Window
Failed Transaction Within 24 hours
Duplicate Payment Within 7 days
Unauthorized Transaction Within 3 days
Service Not Delivered Within 15 days
⚠️ Important: Refund requests submitted after these timeframes may not be accepted.

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please follow these steps:

  1. Contact Support: Email us at business.universewallet@gmail.com
  2. Provide Details: Include the following information:
    • Your registered mobile number
    • Wallet ID
    • Transaction ID
    • Transaction date and time
    • Amount involved
    • Reason for refund request
    • Supporting documents (screenshots, bank statements)
  3. Verification: Our team will verify the transaction details within 3-5 business days
  4. Decision: You will be notified of the refund decision via email

6. Refund Processing Time

Once your refund is approved, please allow the following time for the amount to be credited:

Refund Method Processing Time
Universe Wallet Balance 2-4 hours
Bank Account (NEFT/IMPS) 3-7 business days
UPI 1-3 business days
Debit/Credit Card 5-10 business days

7. Refund Methods

Refunds will be issued using the same method used for the original payment, unless otherwise agreed upon. In some cases, refunds may be issued as:

  • Universe Wallet balance (fastest method)
  • Direct bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS)
  • Original payment method (UPI/Card)

8. Non-Refundable Transactions

⚠️ The following transactions are NOT eligible for refunds:
  • Successfully completed money transfers to other users
  • Service charges and processing fees (unless transaction failed)
  • Amounts spent on games (Dice, Toss, Scratch)
  • Claimed Gift Codes and Lifafa rewards
  • Transactions older than the specified timeframes
  • Refunds requested due to "change of mind"
  • Amounts lost due to user error (wrong mobile number, wrong amount)
  • Transactions involving fraudulent activities

9. Partial Refunds

In certain cases, a partial refund may be issued:

  • If only a portion of the service was delivered
  • If service charges were incorrectly applied
  • If there was a partial failure in a multi-step transaction

10. Failed Transactions

If a transaction fails but money is deducted from your account:

  • The amount will be automatically refunded to your Universe Wallet balance within 2-4 hours
  • If not auto-refunded within 24 hours, contact our support team
  • Provide the transaction ID for faster resolution

11. Chargebacks & Disputes

If you initiate a chargeback through your bank or payment provider:

  • Your Universe Wallet account may be temporarily suspended
  • We will cooperate with the investigation
  • False chargebacks may result in permanent account termination
  • Please contact our support team before filing a chargeback

12. Order Cancellation

Transactions can be cancelled only if:

  • The transaction is still in "pending" status
  • The recipient has not yet claimed the amount
  • Cancellation is requested within 10 minutes of transaction

Once a transaction is completed, it cannot be cancelled. You must request a refund instead.

13. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Refund Policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting on this page. Your continued use of our services after any changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
